Independent Labs · Reimbursement

3 solutions

ToxIQ

Toxicology RCM agent

Pre-submission medical-necessity and coverage checks for drug testing, with definitive/presumptive coding across G0480–G0483. Runs NCCI bundling and frequency-guard logic, then assembles evidence-backed appeals when payers push back.

Explore agents →

MolecuIQ

Molecular & genomic RCM agent

Handles MolDX Z-code registration and test-to-CPT mapping, including NGS tiers 81445 and 81455. Prepares NGS prior-authorization and drafts CO-55 experimental-denial appeals with cited policy.

Explore agents →

Meridian

PAMA rate-cliff model

A deterministic model of the CLFS rate cliff: enter a lab's test mix and it forecasts the 2027–2029 PAMA cuts (up to 15%/yr) and the revenue at risk, and checks the $10k/day reporting-penalty exposure. Runs entirely in the browser — no PHI.
