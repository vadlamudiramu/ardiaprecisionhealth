🧪 Products

Four AI Modules. One Platform.

Each module is purpose-built for a specific lab specialty — not adapted from generic RCM software. Powered by the shared TARA architecture and deployable independently or as a full suite.

ToxIQ™
Toxicology Revenue Intelligence

Purpose-built for independent toxicology labs. Automates denial prevention and appeal generation for urine drug screens (G0480–G0483), definitive drug testing (80300–80377), and specimen validity testing. Addresses the most common tox denial: CO-50 (medical necessity not established) by cross-referencing LCD L36022, patient Rx records, and clinical documentation. Special focus on synthetic opioid detection documentation failures — responsible for 80%+ of tox billing errors.

Urine Drug Screens G0480–G0483 LCD L36022 CO-50 Appeals MAT/OTP Billing
MolecuIQ™
Molecular Diagnostics RCM

The most technically differentiated module. Automates MolDX/DEX Z-code registration management, CPT mapping across 75,000+ genetic tests to under 200 molecular pathology codes, LCD compliance verification, and Technical Assessment tracking. Handles PLA codes (0001U–0400U+), NGS prior authorization under CMS-0057-F, and NCCN/ASCO guideline citation for medical necessity arguments. Covers 7 MAC jurisdictions and commercial payer DEX adoption (UHC, Humana, MVP).

MolDX/DEX Z-codes NGS/PGx CPT 81400–81479 LCD L38337 CMS-0057-F PLA Codes
PulmoIQ™
Pulmonary & Respiratory Intelligence

Longitudinal asthma & COPD intelligence for chronic respiratory care — follows the patient over time and keeps pulmonary-function and sleep-study documentation aligned with coding. Reasons against the GOLD (COPD) and GINA (asthma) guidelines, with air-quality exacerbation triggers. Non-diagnostic — a signal for the clinician, never a diagnosis.

GOLD / GINA PFT & sleep coding Longitudinal Air-quality triggers Chronic care
Aria
Elder Care Companion

A warm, voice-first companion for older adults — gentle daily check-ins, medication and reminder support, a red-flag safety check (stroke FAST, C-SSRS), and emergency escalation to 911 with caregiver notification. Adds Medicare CCM · RPM · TCM care-coordination touchpoints and pairs with the Cadence movement model (95.45% held-out on public UCI HAR). Never a diagnosis.

Voice-first C-SSRS / FAST 911 escalation CCM · RPM · TCM Caregiver

Regulates AI in healthcare and EHR management. Ardia builds compliance natively: human-in-the-loop review, U.S.-only data residency, and patient-facing AI disclosure. For competitors entering Texas, these are expensive retrofits. For Ardia, they're core features from day one.

🤖 TRAIGA — Texas Responsible AI Governance Act (HB 149)

Texas is the third state with comprehensive AI legislation. TRAIGA creates a 36-month regulatory sandbox for AI innovations without standard state licenses — a significant competitive window for Ardia to iterate and establish market position.
