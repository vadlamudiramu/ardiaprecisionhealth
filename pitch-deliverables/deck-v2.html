ARDIA PRECISION HEALTH02
Why Now
A legislated rate cliff hits independent labs in 2027–2029.
  • PAMA / §1834A lets CMS cut Clinical Lab Fee Schedule (CLFS) rates by up to 15% per year across 2027, 2028 and 2029.
  • Compounding: 0.85³ = 0.614 → a cap-bound code lands ~38.6% below its 2025 rate by 2029.
  • Failing to report private-payor data risks a CMP up to $10,000/day.
  • Labs need to model exposure now — and recover every appealable dollar to survive it.
Timing wedge: the pain is scheduled, not speculative
