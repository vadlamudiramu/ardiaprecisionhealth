The reasoning core

One engine behind every module — TARA

Triadic Adjudicative Reasoning Architecture. Every module reuses the same three-layer core; only the policy pack changes. That's why the ecosystem scales without rebuilding the brain each time.

LAYER 1
Symbolic Policy Engine
Deterministic LCD / NCD / MolDX rules, frequency caps and bundling logic — auditable, never hallucinated.
→
LAYER 2
Clinical Reasoning Engine
Claude reads the record against the matched policy, finds the medical-necessity gap, and drafts the argument with citations.
→
LAYER 3
Denial Pattern Library
Payer-specific CARC/RARC playbooks learned from real denial patterns — the appeal angle for each code.

Every module, clearly defined

For each one: the model / software we're building, the use case, the regulatory lane that keeps it compliant, and the concrete codes it works on. Phase tags show what's active now versus scoped for the roadmap.

ToxIQ™
Phase 1 · Active
Toxicology revenue intelligence
Use case
Denial prevention and appeal automation for presumptive and definitive drug testing — the highest-volume denial segment for independent labs.
The model we're building
TARA symbolic policy engine encoding payer LCD necessity, frequency and panel caps, and presumptive→definitive bundling — plus Claude-drafted appeals with EKRA-aware guardrails.
Regulatory lane: administrative / reimbursement safe harbor (21st Century Cures §520(o)(1)). Not a diagnostic device.

The common thread
Why it's an ecosystem, not seven apps
Each module is a policy pack on the shared TARA core. New verticals ship by writing rules, not rebuilding the engine — and every module inherits the same audit trail, de-identification, and human-in-the-loop review.
Reimbursement-first, always. We start where the safe harbor is clearest and only cross into device-regulated detection through the proper FDA pathway.

Roadmap & business plan

Four phases, tied to milestones — not just dates

The lineup rolls out in phases. Phase 1 is a Dallas–Fort Worth launch targeting 2027; international phases are gated on US traction and any required clearances, not calendar dates.

Phase 1 · → 2027
DFW / United States
  • ToxIQ + MolecuIQ active — the flagship reimbursement modules
  • PAMA Framework live as a customer-facing tool
  • Vitamin D/B + PulmoIQ in detection (reimbursement lane first)
  • AcoIQ + BehaviorIQ scoped, reimbursement-safe
Launch market: DFW independent labs. PAMA 2027 rate cliff is the primary adoption driver.
Phase 2 · through 2028
Canada · Mexico · EU
  • Consumer / wellness lines lead international entry
  • MDR + EU AI Act scoping before any EU build
  • Reimbursement engine adapted to each payer landscape
Wellness-first entry keeps early international expansion outside device regulation.
Phase 3 · Q1 2029 →
India · Japan · Singapore
  • Gated on US traction + any required clearances
  • Reimbursement engine follows private-payer density
  • Localized policy packs per market
Sequenced to markets with the clearest private-payer reimbursement structures.
Phase 4 · later
Precision-medicine deepening
  • Deeper precision-medicine capabilities across modules
  • Cardiovascular reimbursement-first entry
  • Detection lanes advanced through FDA pathways as cleared
The long arc: reimbursement infrastructure first, regulated detection only through proper pathways.
