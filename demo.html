What happens under the hood

Every run above passes through the same governed pipeline. Governance lives in code — not in a prompt — which is why it can be audited rather than asserted.

1

De-identify

PHI is stripped before inference. No patient identifiers ever reach the model.

2

Retrieve policy

Pulls the governing LCD/NCD and MolDX/DEX logic — or GOLD/GINA guidance — from a grounded corpus.

3

Reason & gate

Eight deterministic gates run in front of the model and can stop it answering.

4

Cite or abstain

Answers are bound to a real determination with a working cms.gov link. Ungrounded answers are not returned.

5

Human approves

A specialist reviews before anything is filed. Ardia never acts alone.
