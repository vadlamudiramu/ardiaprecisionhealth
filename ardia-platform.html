Reasoning you can audit. Reimbursement you can trust. Care that reaches the people software forgot.

The engine
TARA

TARA Reasoning Engine

Triadic Adjudicative Reasoning Architecture

Ardia's healthcare reasoning core — not a black box. Three layers work together so answers are grounded, not guessed.

  • Denial Pattern Library — payer-specific CARC/RARC playbooks
