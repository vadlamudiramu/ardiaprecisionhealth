Ardia Studio — watch each model reason

Every Ardia model runs on TARA at one of three tiers. Pick a model, pick a tier, send a prompt — and watch the actual trace: the task list it builds, the tools it calls, the policies and evidence it searches and retrieves, then the cited answer.

⚡ Swift fast

Instant answers, low deliberation. No tool calls — a quick, direct response when speed matters.

🧠 Cortex thinking

Step-by-step reasoning. You see the model think the problem through before it answers.

🔬 Scholar deep research

Full investigation — plans, calls tools, searches sources, retrieves policy & evidence, then reasons and cites.

Illustrative demo on synthetic data. Ardia is pre-revenue; these traces are mock illustrations generated in your browser (nothing is uploaded, no real patient data). Tools, codes and sources named are real; outputs are drafts for a human to review. Not a diagnosis.
