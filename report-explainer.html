📄 LUMEN — REPORT EXPLAINER

Your lab report, in plain words.

Paste or upload a lab report and Lumen explains what each value means and what's outside the usual range — so you walk into your appointment already understanding it. It will never tell you what disease you have. That's your clinician's job.

⚠ Research use only · non-diagnostic · your data stays in your browser
lumen://report-explainer● LIVE · in-browser
🔒 Nothing you type or upload leaves this page — it's processed in your browser, and obvious identifiers (phone, email, SSN, dates) are stripped before anything is read.

Paste your values and press Explain my report. I'll walk through each one and flag anything outside the usual range — never a diagnosis.
This is an educational tool, not a diagnosis. Explanations summarize standard adult reference ranges for orientation only; your own lab's reference range and your clinician's judgment always take precedence. An AI that interprets images or reports to identify disease is a regulated medical device — Ardia's diagnostic capability is investigational and gated behind FDA clearance. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Lumen is the plain-language layer of the Ardia roster

Lumen turns lab and scan reports into plain language, and works alongside Aria — Ardia's voice-first elder-care companion for daily tracking, reminders, and emergency alerts — all built HIPAA-first.
